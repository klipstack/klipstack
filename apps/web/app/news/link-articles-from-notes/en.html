A note often points somewhere else: the essay that argued the opposite, or the article that said it first. Until now, only some note fields could link to an article, and on the iPhone the note at the end of an article couldn't link at all.

How it works

  1. In any note, type [[ or @, or use Link article.
  2. Pick one of your recent articles, or search for another.
  3. The link goes into your note, and opening it takes you to that article.

This works in the note at the end of an article, in the Notebook, in Home's note and in highlight notes. On the iPhone, @ opens the picker only at the start of a word, so typing an email address isn't interrupted.
