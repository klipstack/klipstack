A question about a passage sometimes turns into a longer conversation, or the month's questions run out halfway through one. You can now carry it over to the assistant you already use, and it picks up from the same article.

How it works

  1. In the reader, ask about a passage, or chat about the article.
  2. Next to an answer, use Continue in your assistant. In an empty chat, or when no questions are left, use Copy prompt.
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT, Claude or another assistant. The prompt names the article, the passage and the questions you asked, so the assistant can read the article in Klipstack and continue. It leaves out the answers, which can be long.

Your assistant needs to be connected to Klipstack to read the article. If it isn't yet, the prompt says how. Connected assistants are listed in Settings → Connected apps.

Availability

On the web, when AI in the reader is on in Settings → Features and your email is verified. Not in the iPhone app yet.
