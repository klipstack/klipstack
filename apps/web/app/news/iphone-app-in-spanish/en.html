If your iPhone is set to Spanish, Klipstack now speaks it too. The web app already did; the iPhone app was English only until now.

How it works

  1. The app uses the first language in your phone's list that it knows: English or Spanish. Otherwise, English.
  2. To change Klipstack's language without changing your phone's, open the iPhone's Settings, find Klipstack and choose a Language.
  3. Questions you ask about an article are answered in the app's language.

Every screen, menu, alert and VoiceOver label is translated, and so are the reader, the share extension and the widget.

Availability

In the iPhone app, from [CONFIRM: the TestFlight or App Store build that has it]. Your account's language in the web app's Settings still decides the language of the web app and of our emails.

A few rare error messages, such as some sign-in errors, are still in English.
