Someone recommends a post, and it's in Chinese, or Polish. You can now save it anyway: the reader offers to translate it into the language you use Klipstack in, English or Spanish, and keeps the translation for every time you open it.

How it works

  1. Open an article written in another language. Above the text, choose Translate to English. It says how many credits it uses: one for every 1,000 words, started.
  2. The translation takes about a minute, and you can keep reading meanwhile. Then the article shows it, with its title, and you highlight it and ask AI about it as usual.
  3. To read the original, choose Show original text. To go back to it for good, choose Use original language in the article's menu. Translating it again later uses no credits.

If you read a language already, choose Never translate on one of its articles and it won't be offered again. If you always want one translated, choose Always translate on a translated article: the ones you save in it are then translated as they're saved, while your credits last. Articles from your feeds are not. Both lists are in Settings → Features, and your credits this month in Settings → Usage.

Availability

On the web and in the iPhone app (its latest TestFlight build), when Ask AI while reading is on in Settings → Features. Articles up to 20,000 words. In the app, the languages you never or always translate are in Account → Translation, and your credits in Account.
