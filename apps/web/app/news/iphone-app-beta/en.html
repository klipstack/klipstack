Your library now comes with you on your iPhone. Save a link from Safari or any app with a share button, read it on the bus, and the note you write there is waiting on your computer. The app is in public beta, on its way to the App Store, and anyone can join.

How to join

  1. On your iPhone, open the Klipstack beta on TestFlight.
  2. Install TestFlight, Apple's free app for trying apps before they reach the App Store.
  3. Tap Install for Klipstack, then sign in with your Klipstack account.

New to Klipstack? It's invite-only for now, so you need an invite code to create an account, in the app or on the web. If you don't have one, request an invite: people come in a few at a time, so it may take a while.

What's in it

  • Home's next reads, the clean reader, your highlights, and the note at the end of every article.
  • Saving from the share menu of any app.
  • Reading aloud, in English or Spanish, marking each sentence as it goes. The voice runs on your phone, so after a one-time download it works offline.
  • A widget with your next read on the home screen.
  • English and Spanish: the app follows your phone's language.

Availability

iPhone with iOS 17.4 or later. Not on iPad yet. As a beta, it may have rough edges: you can tell us about them from TestFlight, with a screenshot.
