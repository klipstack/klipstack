How to use Klipstack

Klipstack keeps what you want to read, gives you a quiet page to read it on, and remembers what you thought of it. This guide is an article like any other in your library, so you can try each part right here as you read.

Save

Anything with an address can be saved. Paste a link in Save link, use the browser extension (Settings links to it), or share a link to Klipstack from your phone. Klipstack keeps a clean copy of the text, so it stays readable even if the page changes or disappears.

You can also follow the blogs you like in Feed. New posts show up there, and you save the ones you want to read.

Read

Home picks what to read next: the article you are in the middle of, what you saved lately, and older saves worth another look. Library holds everything you saved, newest first.

When you reach the end of an article, it is marked as finished. Finished articles leave Home, and you can archive them to keep the library short.

Highlight

Select a few words and choose Highlight. Try it on the next sentence.

The best ideas are the ones you can find again when you need them.

A highlight stays where you made it. Choose it again to change its color, add a note or delete it.

Write notes

A note on a highlight says why it mattered to you. Try one on the sentence below: what would you want to remember?

Reading is a conversation with someone who isn't in the room.

Every article also has a note of its own, at the end, for what you make of the whole thing. Your notes are private: nobody else reads them.

Find it again

Your highlights and notes collect in the Notebook, article by article. Search covers every word you saved, your highlights and your notes. Narrow a search with filters such as tag:, site: or before:, and press ⌘K (Ctrl K) to jump anywhere.

Tags group articles your way. Add one from an article's menu, or press T.

Share what was worth it

An edition is a collection of good reads with your thoughts attached, published as one link. Mark an article for your next edition, choose a few highlights to quote, add a line or two, and publish it. Your library stays private: only what you put in an edition is public.

Keys

In a list or in an article: S stars, E archives, U marks as unread, T tags, and # deletes. In a list, J and K move between articles and Enter opens one.

What to read next

To try all this on something else, the Save link page has a few short essays on reading and writing, ready to add. Then save the first thing you come across that you want to read properly.
